🏙️ Smart Cities

Smart cities use modern technology to improve the quality of life. These cities use digital systems, automation, and smart infrastructure to manage resources efficiently. The goal is to create a better and more sustainable environment.

Earlier cities were less organised,but now system are highly advanced. Smart traffic systems, waste management and energy-saving technologies are used to improve urban life.

Why Smart Cities?

Smart cities reduce pollution, save energy, and provide better transportation systems. They help citizens live a more comfortable and efficient life. Data-driven decisions improve planning1.
